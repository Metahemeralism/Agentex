# Review forms (typical structure; verify against the current year's reviewer guidelines)

Scales change between years. When the current form is available (call for papers / reviewer guide), use it.

## NeurIPS
Summary · Strengths and weaknesses (originality, quality, clarity, significance) · Questions · Limitations (adequately addressed? societal impact) · Ethics flag
Scores: quality, clarity, significance, originality (1 poor – 4 excellent) · Overall recommendation (recent years: 6-point scale, strong reject → strong accept) · Confidence (1–5)
Also check the NeurIPS paper checklist is completed and consistent with the paper.

## ICML
Summary · Claims and evidence · Methods and evaluation criteria · Theoretical claims (proofs checked?) · Experimental design and analyses · Relation to prior literature · Essential references not discussed · Other strengths and weaknesses · Questions for authors
Overall recommendation (1 reject – 5 strong accept) · Confidence

## ICLR (OpenReview)
Summary · Strengths · Weaknesses · Questions
Soundness, presentation, contribution (1–4) · Rating (1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10) · Confidence (1–5)

## ACL Rolling Review (ACL, EMNLP, NAACL…)
Paper summary · Summary of strengths · Summary of weaknesses · Comments, suggestions and typos
Soundness (1–5) · Excitement (1–5) · Overall assessment (1–5, maps to Findings / main conference) · Reviewer confidence (1–5) · Reproducibility (1–5) · Datasets/software released · Ethical concerns

## CVPR / ICCV / ECCV
Summary · Strengths · Weaknesses · Justification of rating · Additional comments
Rating (reject / weak reject / borderline / weak accept / accept; the scale varies by year) · Confidence

## IEEE conferences and transactions / generic journal
Summary of contribution · Major comments (numbered) · Minor comments (numbered) · Assessment of technical soundness, novelty, presentation, references
Recommendation: accept / minor revision / major revision / reject (and resubmit) · Confidential comments to the editor

## Generic score rubric (when no form is given)
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Strong accept: important, sound, clear; issues are minor |
| 4 | Accept: solid; fixable weaknesses |
| 3 | Borderline: real contribution, but a major issue remains |
| 2 | Reject: a major soundness or novelty problem |
| 1 | Strong reject: wrong, or already known |
